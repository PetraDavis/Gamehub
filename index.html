GAMEHUB

16 free apps for the TI-84 Evo, in one menu.

Plug your calculator into your computer with a USB cable that carries data, turn it on, and press the button. Every app goes straight onto your calculator.

The apps

How to install

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac, Chromebook or Linux). Phones and Safari can't send to a calculator.
  2. Plug the calculator in with a USB cable that can share data (some cables only charge). Turn the calculator on and leave it on the home screen.
  3. Press Send GAMEHUB to my calculator. Your browser asks which device to use: pick the TI-84 Evo and press Connect.
  4. Wait until it says Done. One press sends the menu and every game. Don't unplug the calculator while it sends.

If the button doesn't work

  • Close TI Connect Evo and any other tab that's talking to the calculator, then try again.
  • Try a different cable or USB port.
  • Or do it by hand: press Download all (.zip), unzip it, and drag every .py file onto your calculator with TI Connect Evo.
  • If the calculator says it's out of memory, delete some programs you don't use and send again.

How to play

  • Open the Python app and run GAMEHUB.
  • Arrows move, enter picks, clear goes back.
  • On a game's home screen, press up or down to read how to play.
  • If a button does nothing, open Settings > Key Test in GAMEHUB and tell us which key code it shows.
  • GAMEHUB is the menu, and each game is its own file that GAMEHUB opens when you pick it. That keeps memory free on the calculator. Each game's own Send and Download buttons give you just that game, and it runs on its own.
